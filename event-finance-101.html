About the session

Warwick Wealth Management Society, Warwick Private Equity, Warwick Investment Banking, Warwick Quant, Warwick Hedge Fund Society and Warwick Trading Society joined a panel on routes into finance.

The discussion covered recruitment, key skills and the work involved in different finance careers.
