Questions

How do I become a member?

Use the society’s membership link to open the Square checkout and follow the steps there.

Where are events announced?

Follow WIB on Instagram for event announcements. You can also join the WhatsApp group for society updates.

How do I request the application guide?

Complete the application guide request form. Further application and training links are listed on the resources page.

How can I contact the society?

Use WIB’s official Instagram or LinkedIn page. The committee page lists the current team introduced by the society.
